Transcript and chapters

{% if change %}Review changes{% endif %}

A transcript can come from three places. Captions already on YouTube are read with your sign-in; no AI is involved. For a video file you pick here, Yezatas keeps a temporary copy on this computer while it works and deletes it afterwards. For speech, ffmpeg takes the sound out and {{ tt.asr_model }} on Meta's Model API turns it into text (only the sound is sent, never the picture). For music and sounds, a small model on this computer names what it hears and nothing leaves the computer. Every time in the transcript is a place in the real captions or audio. Chapters are chosen from the transcript and only become part of the draft. Nothing goes to YouTube until you approve and push it.
