Set up the consent screen. It is the page Google shows when you sign in.

  • User type: External.
  • App name: anything, for example Yezatas. Only you will see it. Add your email as the support and developer contact.
  • Add the Google account that owns your channel as a Test user (on the Audience page). Without this, Google blocks the sign-in.
  • Leave the app in Testing. That is fine for personal use, but Google expires the sign-in after 7 days. Then you just sign in again.
